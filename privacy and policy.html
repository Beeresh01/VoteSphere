Privacy & Policy

How we protect and manage your data in the College Online Voting System

Home > Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 1, 2023

1. Introduction

Welcome to the College Online Voting System ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our online voting platform.

By using our voting system, you consent to the data practices described in this policy. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our system.

2. Information We Collect

We collect several types of information from and about users of our voting system:

2.1 Personal Identification Information

  • Full name
  • Student identification number
  • College email address
  • Academic department
  • Year of study

2.2 Voting Information

  • Candidate selections
  • Time and date of voting
  • Authentication logs

2.3 Technical Information

  • IP address
  • Browser type and version
  • Device information
  • System activity logs

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To verify voter eligibility and prevent duplicate voting
  • To administer and conduct the election process
  • To maintain the security and integrity of the voting system
  • To generate anonymous statistical reports about voter participation
  • To troubleshoot technical issues
  • To comply with legal obligations

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information:

  • Encryption of all data in transit and at rest
  • Secure authentication protocols
  • Role-based access controls
  • Regular security audits
  • Anonymization of voting records where possible

Your vote remains anonymous. While we record that you have voted to prevent duplicate voting, we do not store records linking your identity to your specific vote choices.

5. Data Retention

We retain different types of information for different periods:

  • Voting records: Anonymous voting data is retained indefinitely for historical records
  • Authentication logs: 1 year after the election
  • Personal information: 30 days after election results are certified

6. Information Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We may disclose information in these limited circumstances:

  • To election officials for the purpose of administering the election
  • When required by law or legal process
  • To protect the security and integrity of the voting system
  • To prevent or investigate possible wrongdoing

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data we hold
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal data after the election
  • Objection: Object to certain processing activities

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify users of significant changes through the college's official communication channels. Your continued use of the voting system after such modifications constitutes your acknowledgment of the modified policy.
