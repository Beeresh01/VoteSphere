Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know my vote remains anonymous?

VOTESPHERE uses cryptographic separation between voter authentication and ballot submission. While we verify you're eligible to vote, your actual ballot is completely anonymous and cannot be tied back to your identity.

What if I don't have a smartphone or computer?

The university provides voting kiosks in the student union, library, and computer labs during election periods. You can also check out devices from the student affairs office.

How does this compare to traditional paper ballots?

VOTESPHERE is more secure (no lost or miscounted ballots), more accessible (vote from anywhere), more efficient (instant results), and more environmentally friendly (no paper waste). It also provides better voter engagement through candidate profiles and election information.

Can I change my vote after submitting?

During the voting period, you can revisit and change your ballot as many times as you like. Only your final submission before the deadline will be counted.

How are election results verified?

After polls close, the system generates a verifiable audit trail that election officials can review before certifying results. This process maintains anonymity while allowing verification of the vote count accuracy.
